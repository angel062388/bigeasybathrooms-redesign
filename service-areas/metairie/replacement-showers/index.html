Around Metairie

The biggest community in Jefferson Parish, and the homes we work in

Metairie stretches from the New Orleans line to Kenner along the south shore of Lake Pontchartrain, an unincorporated community that is the largest in the parish. A little about it, from things to do to getting around:

01

Things to Do

Walk the lagoons of Lafreniere Park, shop Lakeside on Veterans Boulevard, watch the boats at Bucktown Harbor, and catch the Family Gras and Mardi Gras parades along Veterans each spring.

02

Nearby Suburbs

New Orleans to the east, our home base, Kenner and the airport to the west, and Harahan and River Ridge to the south. Across the lake we also serve Mandeville.

03

Fun Facts

The name comes from the French word for a tenant farm, metairie, from the days when Metairie Road followed the ridge of Bayou Metairie; today it is the largest unincorporated community in Louisiana.

04

Public Transportation

Jefferson Transit buses run along Veterans and Airline and connect with New Orleans RTA lines, I-10 and Causeway Boulevard cross the area, and Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport is about 15 minutes away in Kenner.
